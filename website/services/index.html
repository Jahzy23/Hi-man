HVAC & air duct cleaning services

We specialize in complete HVAC system cleaning for commercial, healthcare, government and industrial facilities. Every job starts with a free inspection.

Core services

Our services include, but are not limited to, the following

AHU / RTU Cleaning

Cleaning the AHU correctly doesn't just mean cleaning your coils. All components such as the blower wheel, blower housing, drain pan and drain lines should always be addressed during a cleaning.

Duct Cleaning

A full inspection is provided free of cost, allowing the opportunity to get to know your system. Regardless of the type of ductwork, whether it be galvanized metal, fiberboard, or flex, a plan will be discussed to remove contaminants at the day and time that best suits your schedule.

Sanitization of HVAC Systems

Following the cleaning of ductwork, some contaminants may remain. In order to successfully remove them, the system may be fogged with an antimicrobial solution.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does an inspection cost?

Nothing. A full inspection, including photographs of your air handlers and ductwork, is provided free of cost so you can see the condition of your system before deciding on any work.

Will cleaning disrupt our operations?

We plan every job around your schedule, including nights, weekends and after-hours work, and we are available 24/7 for emergency service.

Do you clean more than the coils?

Yes. A proper AHU cleaning addresses the blower wheel, blower housing, drain pan and drain lines as well as the coils, and ductwork cleaning covers the full run from the unit to the grills.

What types of ductwork can you clean?

Galvanized metal, fiberboard and flex duct. We can also replace flex, encapsulate fiberboard, install access doors and reline duct interiors and exteriors.

Are you certified?

Our Fresh Air is a NADCA Certified Air Systems Cleaning Specialist and holds Florida HVAC License #CAC1822263. We are fully licensed and insured.
