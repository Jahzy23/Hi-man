Our story

HVAC technicians dedicated to bringing you the clean air we all deserve

Based in Miami and working across the state, Our Fresh Air cleans, refurbishes and sanitizes HVAC systems for some of Florida's most demanding environments: hospitals, county governments, an international airport and hundreds of commercial properties.

We are a NADCA Certified Air Systems Cleaning Specialist and hold Florida HVAC License #CAC1822263. Every project is documented with photographs so our clients always know exactly what was found and what was done.
