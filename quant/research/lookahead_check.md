## Stop and cost structure (descriptive, no P&L)

All columns are BTC / ETH / SOL, signals inside the backtest window.

- **born stopped**: market entries whose stop was already at or beyond the NEXT bar's open. The next open is read by this checker only, never by a strategy.
- **dropped**: signals the module's own safety net removed over the full history because the stop was missing or on the wrong side (warm-up rows). A large number here would mean a logic error hidden by the net.
- **stop distance**: median |entry - stop| as % of price.
- **cost of a stopped trade, % of 1R**: SPEC fees and slippage for entry plus a stop exit, divided by the median stop distance. It is what every losing trade costs on top of the 1R loss, and roughly what a winner must clear first. Tight-stop setups are the expensive ones.
- **3x cap**: share of signals whose stop is closer than 0.167% of price, where the 3x notional cap would shrink the position below the intended 0.5% risk.
