# Setup ideas (untested hypotheses)

29 setups, one module each in `strategies/`. Generated by `research/check_no_lookahead.py` from the modules' own metadata, so this table cannot drift from the code.

- **Nothing here has been backtested.** No P&L was computed and no parameter was tuned: values are textbook or round-number defaults. `PARAM_GRID` in each module lists 2-3 neighbouring values per key for Lab.
- Expect most of these to fail after costs. That is the base rate for trading ideas and it is why Lab exists.
- **Signal count** = rows with `side != 0` inside the backtest window (2022-11-01 to 2026-10-31 15:59 UTC), shown as BTC / ETH / SOL. It is a count of signals, not of trades: the engine holds one position per setup and symbol, so overlapping signals are skipped, and limit orders often do not fill. `tsmom` and `pullback_limit_sma` re-issue their signal every day / every bar by design, which inflates their count.
- **Regime** = the market state the setup is meant to be switched on in (rule in `research/market_ranking.md`).
- `exit` is side-agnostic everywhere it is used: it fires when the state that justified the entry ends (trend state changes, or an oscillator crosses its midpoint), so the same flag is correct for longs and shorts.
- The three BTC lead-lag setups need `ctx['BTCUSDT']` and emit nothing for BTC itself (count 0 is correct).
- Funding setups read `funding_paid` only. The funding archive stops at the end of the backtest window, so they are blind after it until the bot feeds live funding.
- **Survivorship:** BTC, ETH and SOL were not delisted, but they were chosen today knowing they survived. Counts and any later test results on them say nothing about coins that died.

## Notes for Lab (read before testing)

1. **The window has a strong upward drift:** BTCUSDT 20,445.40 -> 77,560.10 (+279%); ETHUSDT 1,569.41 -> 2,249.37 (+43%); SOLUSDT 32.62 -> 95.90 (+194%). Anything long-biased will look good for that reason alone. Compare every setup with a same-side, same-holding-period random-entry baseline, not with zero. Long-only modules: `liq_flush_reclaim`, `liq_flush_retest_bid`, `liq_flush_reversal`, `negative_funding_uptrend`. Short-only: `squeeze_blowoff_fade`.
2. **Multiple testing:** 29 setups x 3 symbols, and PARAM_GRID expands to 639 parameter combinations per symbol (cartesian product, as `lab/validate.py` reads it). Some will pass by luck. A setup should hold up on the neighbouring grid values and on more than one symbol, and BTC/ETH/SOL are highly correlated, so three symbols are far fewer than three independent samples.
3. **Thin samples** (under 100 signals on every symbol in four years): `liq_flush_reclaim`, `liq_flush_retest_bid`, `liq_flush_reversal`, `squeeze_blowoff_fade`, `funding_crowd_unwind`, `funding_extreme_fade`, `oi_divergence_fade`. Whatever these show, the confidence interval will be wide.
4. **Costs:** `research/lookahead_check.md` lists what a stopped trade costs in units of risk for each setup. The tight-stop 1h setups pay 10-25% of 1R per losing trade before any edge.
5. **Open-interest data has holes:** the archive prints OI = 0 on some hours (BTCUSDT 0, ETHUSDT 0, SOLUSDT 0) and at least one partial print just before a zero (ETH, bar opening 2026-10-15 11:00 UTC: -18% in one bar with price flat). Modules that use OI drop the zeros themselves; the multi-bar ones also take a trailing 3-bar median. `strategies/data.py` does not clean this.
6. **Liquidations are a proxy.** Binance publishes no liquidation history; 'flush' means big bar + volume spike + OI drop on one 1h bar. A cascade that straddles the hour boundary can be missed.
7. **Same-row exit and entry:** `donchian_trend` and `tsmom` can carry `exit=True` and the opposite `side` on one row. Both re-issue their entry on later bars, so nothing is lost if the engine does not reverse on a single bar. `ma_trend_atr_stop` puts the new entry on the bar after the cross for the same reason.
8. **Limit entries** are always at or better than the signal close, and the stop is measured from the limit. If the next bar opens beyond the limit the fill price is the engine's decision.
9. **Regime labels per day** come from `research/ranking.py: regime_series(load(sym, '1h'))`. The label stamped on day D uses data to the close of D, so apply it from D+1.
10. **Session setups** use fixed UTC hours. US daylight saving moves the cash open between 13:30 and 14:30 UTC; `ny_open_drive` covers both with a two-bar window, the others ignore DST.
